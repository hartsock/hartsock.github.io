# About

This is the personal site of **Shawn Hartsock** — a home for essays and short
series on intelligence, systems, and the ideas that tend to outlast the tools
that carry them.

Everything here is written in plain text and kept in
[git](https://github.com/hartsock/hartsock.github.io). The words belong to the
reader and the writer, not to a platform — so where pieces are syndicated
elsewhere, this is the canonical copy.
