I just gave the [Groovy Integration Patterns](http://gr8conf.us/Presentations/Groovy-Integration-Patterns) talk at [GR8Conf.us](http://gr8conf.us/) otherwise known as the *Grobnicating Frobnicator *talk. Where I take us on a journey through techniques on introducing Groovy into Enterprisey environments, along the way I take a really brief foray into the "Planet of the Monkey Patches" where we discuss some interesting interactions you can do with Groovy embedded in a mostly Java application. Then we move into the world of multi-tennant Java applications that can leverage Groovy and Groovy DSL to swap out function in configuration or dynamically at run-time.

 The code is over at[https://github.com/hartsock/groovy-integration-patterns](https://github.com/hartsock/groovy-integration-patterns)
